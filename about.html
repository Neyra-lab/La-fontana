Our Story

Family recipes, fresh ingredients and a table with room for everyone.

Born from a Family Table.

La Fontana started in a small kitchen where Sunday lunch lasted all afternoon. Those meals taught us that good food is simple: ripe tomatoes, good olive oil, fresh pasta and time.

Today we bring that same spirit to our dining room. Whether you are here for a quick lunch or a long celebration, we want you to feel looked after.

What We Believe In

Fresh every day

We shop each morning and cook with what is best that day, so the menu always tastes of the season.

Made by hand

Our pasta is rolled, cut and shaped in the kitchen. Sauces, breads and desserts are made from scratch.

Shared at the table

Dishes are made to pass around. We would rather you try a little of everything than order alone.

From Our Kitchen

Our chefs begin before sunrise, kneading dough and starting the slow sauces that need hours to develop. Nothing is rushed, and nothing leaves the pass unless we would happily eat it ourselves.

Ask us about the dish of the day. It is usually the one we are most excited about.
