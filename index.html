A Tradition of Taste, A Passion for Italy.

La Fontana began with a simple idea: cook the way our grandmothers did, with good ingredients, patience and generous hands. Every sauce is simmered slowly and every pasta is shaped in our own kitchen.

We believe a meal is more than food. It is time spent with the people you love, and we set our tables to make that time last.

Authentic Italian Flavors, Crafted with Love.

From crisp bruschetta and hand-rolled tagliatelle to slow-braised ossobuco and a proper tiramisù, our menu follows the seasons and the traditions of the Italian table.

Every dish is made to order with fresh, honest ingredients, and meant to be shared.
